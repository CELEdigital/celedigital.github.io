During the [14th Edition of the CELE Workshop](https://celeup.org/es/taller/), the first issue of *TTG Scan LatAm*, the new publication from TTG Forum, was presented in Buenos Aires. Titled *Urgent Conversations on Latin America’s Digital Sovereignty*, it brings together eight thinkers from Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Mexico, and Uruguay, who were invited to offer a comprehensive overview of technology, trade, and geopolitics in the region.<!-- TODO: link to the publication when the link is available. -->

The call for contributions was deliberately open-ended: there was no assigned topic or specific prompt on what to write. The essays cover payment systems, taxation, international trade rules, digital identity, and the extraction of data, minerals, and water. The case of Pix, Brazil’s instant payment system, illustrates how quickly a domestic policy decision can become a geopolitical issue. The overarching conclusion of the publication is that digital sovereignty requires economic power, political decisions, and a seat at the table where the rules are written. From different perspectives, the articles agree on the need to rethink digital sovereignty and strengthen regional cooperation.

The presentation was led by Carolina Botero of TTG Forum and was followed by a panel featuring two of the authors, Alejandro and Vinicius, moderated by Agustina Del Campo, director of CELE. It was a pleasure to participate in the launch and welcome so many partner organizations from the region to this conversation.

The English edition is now available and freely accessible; the Spanish and Portuguese versions will be published soon.
