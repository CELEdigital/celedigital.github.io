The Center for Studies on Freedom of Expression (CELE) at the University of Palermo School of Law and the Center for Human Rights (CDH) at the University of Buenos Aires School of Law organized the event **“How to Protect Freedom of Expression and Access to Information in Armed Conflicts?”**.

Chantal Joris, Head of Law & Policy at [ARTICLE 19](https://www.article19.org/), led the presentation and opened the discussion, which centered largely on a recent Article 19 report on [this issue](https://www.article19.org/free-expression-war-conflict/), which seeks to safeguard freedom of expression amid the dynamics of armed conflict, within the framework of both international human rights law and international humanitarian law. Leonardo Filippini, from the CDH, offered comments. 

The Article 19 report is titled *Clearing the Fog of War: Protecting Freedom of Expression in Armed Conflict*. It can be found [here](https://www.article19.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Clearing-the-Fog-of-War-3-December-2024.pdf).
