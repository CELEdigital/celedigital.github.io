Since December 2020, CELE has organized a bimonthly virtual seminar on freedom of expression, where works in progress or recently published are discussed. While in its first year the focus was on academic production in Latin America, since 2022 the seminar has included global topics. The seminar is an academic space for discussion: papers are read in advance and discussed in a collegial setting for one hour.
