abstract: The article examines the transparency and labelling obligations that apply to advertising content created with generative artificial intelligence, focusing on Article 50 of the European Union's Artificial Intelligence Act. It reviews the frameworks already governing commercial communications — misleading advertising, unfair commercial practices, online advertising regulation, and industry self-regulation — and analyses the distinction between the machine-readable marking required under Article 50.2 and the public disclosure of deepfakes required under Article 50.4, together with the limited exception for creative, satirical, artistic, or fictional works.
