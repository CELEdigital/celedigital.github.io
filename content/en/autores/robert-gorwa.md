Robert is a researcher at the WZB Berlin Social Science Center, where he studies the political economy of platform governance. He holds a DPhil in Politics from the University of Oxford and is the author of *The Politics of Platform Regulation: How Governments Shape Online Content Moderation* (Oxford University Press, 2024), a book on the formal and informal ways governments shape online content moderation. He took part in CELE's online symposium on regulatory threats with the post ["Political Capital and Informal Platform Governance"](/en/posts/political-capital-and-informal-platform-governance/), and in CELE's 2024 seminar series.
