We kicked off the [14th Edition of the CELE Workshop](https://celeup.org/es/taller/) with a presentation on the TTG Forum by our friend Carolina Botero. The workshop serves as an annual gathering for an ever-growing digital rights community in Latin America, which this year included—in a special way—activists defending the rights of children and adolescents, as well as organizations combating online violence against women.
